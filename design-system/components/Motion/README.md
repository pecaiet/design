# Motion

How things move in every app: one speed, one curve, three kinds of motion, the same everywhere.

- Speed (token): `pc-motion`, 120ms, for everything that moves. Curve: Ease out (`--pc-motion-ease`). Worked out from it: `--pc-motion-wait` (a tooltip's wait, ×3, 360ms), `--pc-motion-spin` (one spinner turn, ×7, 0.84s), `--pc-motion-slide` (unknown progress, ×12, 1.44s), `--pc-motion-pulse` (the skeleton, ×13, 1.56s).
- Change: hover and focus colours fade, ticks and dots fade in, arrows turn (accordion, tree, Select), an icon swapped in a button (a copy's tick, the spinner at work) fades in.
- Then three kinds, the same everywhere and never two at once (nothing fades while it moves):
- Fade, for what appears in place: menus, popovers, tooltips (after 360ms), dialogs, tab panels, carousel slides, chips added or removed and content that takes a skeleton's place fade in and out where they are.
- Grow, for what takes room or gives it back: toasts and field errors whenever they appear; a row or alert the person just added (`Pecaiet.enter(el)`, or a Turbo stream that adds it); accordion answers and tree branches as they open and close; whatever is removed (`Pecaiet.leave(el)`). It fades while it grows or shrinks, in one motion, so what's around it glides instead of jumping (pecaiet.js). Content that's only drawn again (a Turbo frame, a React update) shows at once, never row by row. Table rows can't grow: they fade.
- Slide, for what comes from an edge or travels: the drawer, the sidebar and its phone sheet, the sheets on phones and the skip link come in from their edge (`--pc-enter`), while the page dims behind sheets; the switch's knob, the segmented pill, the tab line and a progress bar slide to their new place.
- Loading: the spinner turns, the skeleton pulses, unknown progress slides. Pages crossfade (Chrome, Edge, Safari); jumps within a page scroll smoothly.
- Still: numbers, the stepper's number, a pressed button.
- Off: Settings › Preferences › Motion (On · Off) puts `data-motion="off"` on `<html>`, remembered in the motion cookie (the layout sets it before the page draws, like the theme). Until someone chooses, a device that asks for less motion gets Off. Off makes everything instant; the spinner still turns, slowly (2.4s).
- `pecaiet.js` (next to `pecaiet.css`) plays what CSS can't. By itself: closings of menus, popovers and sheets when the app hides them, Escape on a dialog, accordion answers and tree branches growing and shrinking, toasts and field errors growing in, icons swapped in a button and content that replaces a skeleton fading in, an alert's x and any `[data-pc-dismiss]`, the tab line, Turbo removals, smooth jumps, and radios named `pc-theme` or `pc-motion`. One call for what only the app knows: `Pecaiet.leave(row)` before removing something (React), `Pecaiet.close(dialog)`, `Pecaiet.setTheme(mode)`, `Pecaiet.setMotion(mode)`, `Pecaiet.enter(el)`.
- See it: this card plays each kind on the real components (Play, or Play all). Every motion, one card each, with Slow ×5 and Off: the [motion playground](https://claude.ai/artifact/Wg3LnYi3P4z6BptwdCDzqZ). Here `pecaiet.js` is the design system's bundle (`components/bundle.js`, built from it), so every card plays the same motion as the apps.
- Never: durations or curves of an app's own, a whole page animating in, bounce or shake, numbers counting up, loops for decoration.
