# Motion

How things move in every app: one speed, one curve, three kinds of motion, the same everywhere.

- Speed (token): `pc-motion`, 120ms, for everything that moves. Curve: Ease out (`--pc-motion-ease`). Worked out from it: `--pc-motion-wait` (a tooltip's wait, the same 120ms), `--pc-motion-spin` (one spinner turn, ×7, 0.84s), `--pc-motion-slide` (unknown progress, ×12, 1.44s), `--pc-motion-pulse` (the skeleton, ×13, 1.56s).
- Change: hover and focus colours fade, ticks and dots fade in and out, arrows turn (accordion, tree, Select, Combobox), an icon swapped in a button (a copy's tick, the spinner at work, a sort arrow) or at a field's end (a search's spinner, then its x) fades in.
- Then three kinds, the same everywhere and never two at once (nothing fades while it moves):
- Fade, for what appears in place: menus, popovers, tooltips (after 120ms), dialogs, tab panels, carousel slides, chips added or removed, content that takes a skeleton's place and a picture once it has loaded fade in and out where they are.
- Grow, for what takes room or gives it back: toasts and field errors whenever they appear; a row or alert the person just added (`Pecaiet.enter(el)`, or a Turbo stream that adds it); accordion answers and tree branches as they open and close; whatever is removed (`Pecaiet.leave(el)`), a fixed field's error included. It fades while it grows or shrinks, in one motion, so what's around it glides instead of jumping (pecaiet.js). Content that's only drawn again (a Turbo frame, a React update) shows at once, never row by row. Table rows can't grow: they fade.
- Slide, for what comes from an edge or travels: the drawer (beside the page too, the page narrowing with it), the sidebar and its phone sheet, the sheets on phones and the skip link come in from their edge (`--pc-enter`), while the page dims behind sheets; the switch's knob, the segmented pill, the tab line and a progress bar slide to their new place.
- Loading: the spinner turns, the skeleton pulses, unknown progress slides. Pages the browser loads crossfade (Chrome, Edge, Safari); a page an Inertia app swaps in shows at once, going back the same as forward. Jumps within a page scroll smoothly.
- Still: numbers, the stepper's number, a pressed button, and the same view with new data (a new month in the date picker, rows sorted or filtered again): it shows at once.
- Off: Settings › Preferences › Motion (On · Off) puts `data-motion="off"` on `<html>`, remembered in the motion cookie (the layout sets it before the page draws, like the theme). Until someone chooses, a device that asks for less motion gets Off. Off makes everything instant; the spinner still turns, slowly (2.4s).
- `pecaiet.js` (next to `pecaiet.css`) plays what CSS can't. By itself: closings of menus, popovers and sheets when the app hides them, Escape on a dialog, accordion answers and tree branches growing and shrinking, toasts and field errors growing in, icons swapped in a button and content that replaces a skeleton fading in, an alert's x and any `[data-pc-dismiss]`, the tab line, Turbo removals, smooth jumps, and radios named `pc-theme` or `pc-motion`. One call for what only the app knows: `Pecaiet.leave(row)` before removing something (React), `Pecaiet.close(dialog)`, `Pecaiet.setTheme(mode)`, `Pecaiet.setMotion(mode)`, `Pecaiet.enter(el)`.
- See it: this card plays each kind on the real components (Play, or Play all). Every motion, one card each, with Slow ×5 and Off: the [motion playground](https://claude.ai/artifact/Wg3LnYi3P4z6BptwdCDzqZ). Here `pecaiet.js` is the design system's bundle (`components/bundle.js`, built from it), so every card plays the same motion as the apps.
- Never: durations or curves of an app's own, a whole page animating in, bounce or shake, numbers counting up, loops for decoration.
