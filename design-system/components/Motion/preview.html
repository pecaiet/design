On a phone
Loading: the spinner turns (0.84s), the skeleton pulses (1.56s), unknown progress slides (1.44s). They loop by themselves.
Off (Settings, or a device that asks for less motion): all of it at once; the spinner turns slowly. Everything here is the real component, played by the bundle (pecaiet.js), as in the apps. Every motion, one card each: the motion playground (linked in the guideline).
