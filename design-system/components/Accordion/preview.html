What is a pe caiet account?
One account for every pe caiet app: sign in once and move between them.
I didn't get the reset email
Check your spam folder, then make sure you typed the address you signed up with. You can ask for a new link from the sign-in page.
How do I sign out of a device?
Open Settings, then Sessions, and sign it out there.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, from Settings, Account. It can't be undone.
